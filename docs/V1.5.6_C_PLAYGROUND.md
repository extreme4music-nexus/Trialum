# TRIALUM 1.5.6-C — animated skills and offline solo Playground

**Delivery:** source + automated test phase, not the final 1.5.6 release. A future 1.5.6-D will add consent-based shared playground and friends' visits.

## Playground and progression
- The solo Playground appears in **More → Игротека**. No online identity or cloud service is required.
- Retrospective unlock: selected pet at evolution **level 15** AND research laboratory **level 4**. Works for existing saved worlds; adds `s.playground` lazily without resetting any pet, habitat, credits, research or original scene.
- Starter equipment: **sandbox** and **seesaw**, both free. Slide level20/lab4 (16 earned credits), pool level25/lab5 (25), obstacle course level35/lab8 (38), trampoline level45/lab9 (52).
- Space tier1 supports 3 equipment slots, the two starter toys plus one. Upgrade with earned credits for each extra slot, max 5 expansion tiers. All unlocks cost credits obtained through ordinary game actions. There is no purchase system, billing, ad, tracking, paywall or premium-only toy.
- One dedicated playable Canvas scene shows terrain, six animated equipment types, creature movement, splash, leap and skill effects. Each pet may earn practice XP for **at most four activities per simulated day**; visual replay is unlimited but earns nothing. Energy requirement and reduced-motion setting are respected.
- Skill practice can increase an **already learned** skill, up to its established cap. It never skips progression gates for symbols, logic or dialogue. Training in the main Pets screen immediately demonstrates the learned action without another action cost.
- **Interactive symbols and logic**: simple choice puzzle appears if the pet already knows that skill. Wrong answers incur no penalty or reward. Correct answer counts as a single gameplay activity.
- **Interactive dialogue**: once trained, select from three short fictional pet responses. No general-purpose chat or LLM prompt required. All pet exchanges are local.
- Classic AND advanced aquarium render paths show wave, spin, leap, symbols, logic and dialogue with distinct animated effects. The playground uses the creature's 2.5D art where available and a simple classic fallback otherwise.

## Save and security boundaries
- `s.playground` is local world progress only, eligible for existing local encrypted/native JSON backup; **never** stores a social credential.
- The whole offline world remains functional with no Wi-Fi/internet and without `TRIALUM_SOCIAL_ENABLED`.
- A client-only save is not trusted as a source of online rewards or collaborative changes. 1.5.6-D MUST create separate authoritative server rules, idempotent ledgers and consent checks. Do not transfer client-earned XP directly into ranked social accounts.
- All free-earned credits are deducted atomically within local synchronous action to avoid repeated unlock, and a limited activity count prevents replay farming. No external ads, behavioral analytics or tracking IDs.
