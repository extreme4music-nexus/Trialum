**Recorded 2026-11-15.** **Status: FUTURE PRODUCT PROPOSAL ONLY — NOT IMPLEMENTED, NOT YET TECHNICALLY APPROVED.**

## Original proposal in scope
Build a spatial mini-city/neighborhood as a dedicated living place for pets belonging to **multiple consenting players**, not one player's isolated habitat:
- Streets, houses, apartment buildings and housing blocks, parks, open-air playgrounds and other shared outdoor spaces.
- A 3D neighborhood map with navigable roads and locations, the separate gateway for pets/homes/outdoor community life.
- Social pet encounters, shared play and compatible visits across different players' households, subject to persistent pet identity, ownership, friendships, presence and consent rules already established in Trialum.
- The **main central Lobby** is now the universal hub for all other 3D public modules (laboratories, research, flora, food production and future facilities); **pet homes** are the explicit exception and will be reached by the new mini-city/neighborhood entrance when that world is built. No retroactive relocation of saved pets now.
- The concrete geography, network/instance model, ownership/lease, park interactions, child-friendly contact permissions, UI/map and technical rollout need a separate owner-led design conversation when this milestone becomes active.

## Important constraints and unresolved architecture
- “All players” is a product aspiration to share a living world, **NOT** authorization for a global public chat, exposing real names/addresses/GPS, making every user's private pets visible without consent, or centralizing all world history.
- At the later phase, evaluate bounded neighborhoods/rooms/shards, friend- or opt-in visibility, data minimization, presence authority, anti-duplication, offline continuation, safe pet return and server security before committing to real-time all-player scale.
- The local simulation remains offline-first and private. Shared space needs authenticated, consented server-authoritative transitions; don't send private per-pet memory, home location or real-world location to other players.
- Anti-abuse/moderation, safety for children, reporting/blocking and public-facing content filters are mandatory design gates.
- The future mini-city is not a license to change Debian, SQL schema, API, FCM settings, ownership rules, Android package identities or live user data now.
- Preserve immutable pet IDs, history, bonds, skills, backup compatibility, all existing accepted LW-A/B/C transitions.
- The owner's prior rule still holds: **complete the 3D skeleton across the entire game first**, then undertake detailed final models/graphics/animations according to fresh owner directions. A prototype neighborhood should not become an unapproved new graphics overhaul.

## Release gates (future, not executable in this phase)
1. Owner approves the city concept and network/visibility model.
2. Small isolated 3D city-map prototype with houses/park/outdoor playground and no real account data changes.
3. Controlled two-device invitation/private-neighborhood tests, no duplicates and safe return.
4. Offline/reconnect/long-absence tests and load/performance validation.
5. Child-safety, social/privacy review and full opt-in UI on real devices.
6. Explicit owner acceptance before any merge or public visibility.
