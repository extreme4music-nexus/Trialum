## Ground rules

- `OWNER PASS`: owner observed physically. `CI PASS`: repeatable named workflow on exact SHA. `INDEPENDENT PASS`: reviewer tested separately. `ACCEPTED`: all required checks plus explicit owner approval. Keep these distinct.
- Do not reopen passing LLM functionality just because older release checklists say "untested." A future model performance/battery evaluation is a separate requirement.
- No edits to owner's Ubuntu production server, secrets, NAS, router, Firebase console or device from documentation. No destructive Docker volume commands. Store no credentials in GitHub.
- Work one verified item at a time. Keep failed cases as actionable reproduction, expected behavior, actual result, fix SHA, test names and evidence. Do not make a feature claim from a design document.
- The owner intentionally postpones Play Console account setup, public beta and release; treat them as separate future publication gates, *not a reason to force rushed release*. However unresolved privacy, consent, security, data-loss and age-appropriate safety tests remain blockers for feature completion where applicable.
