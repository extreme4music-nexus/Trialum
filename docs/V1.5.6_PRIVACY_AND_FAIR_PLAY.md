# TRIALUM — zero ads / minimal-data / earnable monetization contract

Accepted product policy 2026-11-08. Applies to TRIALUM client, Social Server, analytics choices, store listing and future partners. Do NOT call a release privacy-safe before independent inspection.

## Absolute prohibitions
- No external advertising, third-party ad network integrations, behavioral or cross-app trackers, third-party ad IDs, profiling for ads, trading or selling user data to data brokers, sponsored player feeds, or sale of in-game messages to anybody.
- No sharing data with advertising intermediaries. Internal release announcements can be displayed without impression measurement or individualized targeting.
- No compulsory online accounts to play solo; no account by real name, email, phone, contacts permission, approximate/precise GPS, advertising identifier, camera or microphone to enter the playground.
- No purchase-only game power or hidden probabilities for paid loot boxes. No pay-to-win ranked boosts.

## Privacy truthfulness: what is and is not 'zero'
Online friend pairing, an offline invitation, offline delivery of a short message, shared construction and abuse prevention necessarily require minimal operational records. There cannot be literally zero processing while these features work. Collect only pseudonymous installation/public identity, invitation hash/expiry, friend consent links, playground memberships, pet animation events, contribution ledger, pending notification references, opt-in push delivery token and short message until delivered/expired. Transient network metadata may be visible to host/tunnel operators; treat as personal data and restrict logging. Never store raw IP unnecessarily in application records. Do not use message text for analytics, ranking or ads.
FCM/Firebase uses Firebase installation identifiers/device registration tokens and Google is a subprocesser. Push is optional, explicit, and must be disclosed correctly in Google Play Data Safety and privacy policy. Push message body is NOT put in generic notification payload. FCM isn't built-in end-to-end encryption.

## Data lifecycle (targets, validate in implementation)
- Invitations: 15 minutes, hashed, single use; expire/delete automatically.
- Pending simple messages: 7 days max, then purge, or delete immediately on delivery/read if no retention requested.
- Delivery tokens: only while opted in; delete on opt-out and revoke.
- Game social account: export and delete endpoints, individual friendship and playground leave/removal. Deletion must scrub social content/associations subject to narrowly documented anti-abuse and ledger integrity requirements.
- Shared contributions: ledger must be audit-friendly while group exists, but avoid personal identity fields; pseudonymous membership IDs. A migration/deletion policy must be implemented before public release.
- Encrypted storage transport; database encryption/backups and strict secret access. No secrets in Git, crash diagnostics or support emails. Backups with explicit retention and verified restore.
- Logs: no request bodies, push tokens, invite codes, auth headers or message text; minimised retention. No device fingerprinting.

## Messaging safety
Short 1:1 and visit-related messages only, no global chat, block/report/rate limiting, per-person notification mute and deletion. User-generated free text requires age-appropriate safety strategy and moderation before public minors deployment; start with preset phrases for closed beta.

## Earnable economy (AFTER 1.5.6, not shipped now)
Every purchasable thing has a published nonpaying unlock path: cosmetics, slots, building resources, tokens, XP packs, extra activities, workshop bundles. Pricing/effort ranges must be disclosed; never make unpaid play intentionally impossible. Paid accelerators cannot bypass safety, social consent, ownership, rate limits or competitive fairness; paid and earned balances must be auditable and server-authoritative for online uses.
Android Play distribution of in-app digital goods generally requires Google Play Billing. Never accept a client-reported purchase as proof; validate token/acknowledge purchase and refund state server-side, make entitlement idempotent. No purchase UI or billing SDK in 1.5.6.
Store descriptions must be truthful about networking, push, Google services, and optional on-device models.
Future transfer to cloud/domain changes the public API origin only, not privacy commitments.
