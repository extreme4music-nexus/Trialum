# LW-C v2 — Real-time autonomous play, not a 30-minute reward timer

**Owner decision 12 Nov 2026:** Sending a pet to the playground must immediately feel like living, independent behavior. Waiting 6–22 minutes for a first interaction is rejected. This supersedes the earlier 30–110 minute reward/checkpoint model in [ADR-03](LW_C_ADR03_AUTONOMY_20261112.md) and the initial [0/4 diagnosis](LW_C_0_OF_4_DEVICE_FINDING_20261112.md). The owner expects immediate local and shared room interactions, with spontaneous two-pet dialogue.

## Implemented on PR #42

- **First local action at +4 seconds**, then an autonomous interaction every **12 seconds** while the pet is legitimately in the local playground. App reconciles on a **3-second visible-only tick**, and also on load, restore and foreground. It never awards XP per rendered frame.
- There are **up to four reward-eligible events per session and four rewarded exercises per pet/game day shared with manual practice**. The initial learned/unlocked-eligible practice normally gives +4 XP within seconds (unless daily cap or energy gate is already exhausted). After rewards cap, the pet **keeps playing, resting, exploring and communicating**; the screen shows continuous interaction count and latest action, rather than incorrectly labeling four rewarded practices as *all* autonomous behavior.
- **Continuous and player-directed visit**: a local visit lasts until the owner returns the pet home. The existing **2-hour LW-A presence TTL** only remains a finite safety return for crashes and offline orphaned sessions. It does **not** control reward timing. No 30–110-minute waiting rule.
- **Offline finite-safe persistence:** version 2 autonomous session records have deterministic seed, capped XP ledger, nextActionAt, total interaction count and 16-entry recent activity log. An old v1 session is upgraded only when its still-matching live local presence is confirmed, retaining prior awarded XP/ledger, without retroactive replay. A stopped/expired session never awards again. Offline catch-up is bounded to eight decisions per foreground load to prevent hundreds of loops or unintended large XP.
- **Local companions:** when two pets are explicitly present in the same local playground, they visibly play together and alternate coherent question/answer reactions every ~4 seconds; a solo pet still reacts/observes. These are **rule-driven simulated creature utterances**, not free-text messages or guaranteed natural-language LLM intelligence. The model does not learn a locked skill.
- **Shared room companions:** only **authenticated, server-confirmed live occupant pet profiles** count. The shared scene now moves and assigns toy actions; with at least two confirmed pets, both clients independently derive the same bounded question/answer exchange from room identity + turn timestamp and show it on the scene and an accessible caption. No new network messages, private chat, inferred friend availability or client-authored score.
- **Group score remains server-authoritative** and is earned via the existing confirmed button action (one group point, daily max six). Local evolution XP is distinct. Simulated conversations do not grant group currency and do not send messages to other users.
- Animation and caption timers work only when the Android WebView is visible. No server or database migrations, no Firebase changes, no forced new app permissions, no Play release, no ads, no loss of the owner's backed-up worlds. Existing permanent owner keys remain pinned.

## Acceptance gates

1. Deterministic **first-action <10 seconds**, then repeated 12-second interactions and independent dialogue even after the four per-day rewards have been spent. Confirm two local pets can take turns and no XP is generated by opening the scene or reading speech.
2. Save/restore and v1-to-v2 migration: previously earned XP and prior events are preserved, never replayed; clock rollback, interrupted network, all already-unlocked toy gates and atomic presence return are respected.
3. Shared scene: 0 or 1 verified occupant means no invented second pet; with 2 or more verified occupants dialogue begins immediately. Room access, membership and existing server-authoritative scoring cannot be spoofed.
4. CI: Android JS/UI, English catalog, social/server/security, signed-package identity, 1/10/100 sessions. Run internal APKs over installed v30 preserving same package signer/identity; **versionCode 31, 1.6-LW-dev.8** (unless newer app was actually published).
5. Owner physically tests all three signed APKs and reports PASS before LW-C is accepted and before LW-D starts. Independent wider release audit remains separate.

**This is still private QA, not Google Play submission, and rule-based short pet speech is not a claim of local LLM-powered independent thought.**
